Adds an optional `limit` argument to `Table.search()`.

With `limit`, the scan stops early and the partial result is not cached (a cached entry must always be the complete result for its query, C3/ADR-3). If the full result is already cached, it is sliced. Without `limit` behaviour is unchanged.

Tests: `tests/test_search_limit.py`, including the interaction between limited and unlimited searches and the query cache. Full suite passes.
